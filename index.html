Blimat - Account & Data Deletion Request

Users can request deletion of their Blimat account and associated data by contacting us.

Please email us at:

dev.thecamera@gmail.com

Use this email subject: Delete my Blimat account

Please include the email address used to sign in to Blimat.

Data that may be deleted

  • Account profile data
  • Parent onboarding answers
  • Child birth year or age-related app setup data
  • App progress and settings connected to the account
  • Account identifiers connected to the app where possible

Data that may be kept

Some purchase, subscription, billing, tax, fraud prevention, or legal records may be kept by Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat, or other payment providers when required.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are usually completed within 30 days.
